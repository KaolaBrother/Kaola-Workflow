# D-804-01. An undelivered disjointness verdict is not a proven overlap: one classifier, one bounded re-probe, one honest label

Date: 2026-07-26
Status: Accepted
Issue: #804
Related: #802 / `D-802-01` (the principle this applies: repair a removable blocker before degrading;
         never let uncertainty impersonate evidence); #599 (fixed this exact classification at the
         speculative selector — the fix that never reached the normal co-open site); #616 (the
         `serialDegradeReason` telemetry channel this completes)

## Context

`tryFormLaneGroup` returned `{ ok:false, reason:'overlapping_write_sets' }` for **any** non-ok
`--parallel-safe` result — a genuine overlap report, but equally a crashed subprocess, unparseable
JSON, or an exec fault. The caller then serial-degraded the whole frontier with **no**
`serialDegradeReason` label at all.

Two distinct wrongs compounded:

1. **Misclassification.** A transient machinery fault was recorded as `overlapping_write_sets` — a
   *proven exact-path overlap*, the one serializer the doctrine accepts without question. The
   doctrine's evidence bar ("serial requires present-tense, checkable evidence for a named
   serializer") was being satisfied by a **label**, not by evidence: nobody proved an overlap; the
   prover never delivered a verdict at all.
2. **Invisibility.** The degrade was unlabeled, so a persistently-failing validator subprocess
   (ENOBUFS on a huge frontier, an environment fault) would silently serialize every write frontier
   forever — the exact failure mode the `parent_dirty` telemetry work shipped to prevent, alive on the
   adjacent branch.

The asymmetry was already acknowledged in-tree: `selectSpeculativeWriteGroup` had been fixed to
distinguish exactly this. The normal site never received the same fix — the two sites drifted.

## Decision

### One classifier, shared verbatim by both sites

`classifyParallelSafeVerdict(ps)` is the single reader of a `--parallel-safe` result:

- `ok` — `exitCode === 0 && result === 'ok'`: a delivered, clean verdict.
- `overlap` — a non-ok verdict carrying a **well-formed `overlapping` array** (even empty): a real
  overlap *report*. Legitimate serializer.
- `indeterminate` — a non-ok verdict **without** one: the prover delivered no verdict about anything.

Both `tryFormLaneGroup` and `selectSpeculativeWriteGroup` call it through one probe helper, so the two
sites can never drift again.

### Exactly one bounded re-probe, then fail closed

`probeParallelSafe` runs the probe and, on `indeterminate`, re-runs it **exactly once**. The probe is
read-only and idempotent (a pure question about declared sets in the frozen plan), and the plausible
cause class of an indeterminate verdict is transient — a signal, ENOBUFS, a scheduler hiccup — so
re-asking once is the cheapest repair of a removable blocker. A second indeterminate is final.

**Two outcomes, no retry loop.** A proven overlap (or a clean `ok`) never re-probes: re-asking a
settled question is pure waste.

**Fail-closed stays fail-closed.** An indeterminate verdict never co-opens. The doctrine's "uncertain
overlap co-opens under the retained net" governs a *delivered* verdict about unresolvable write-set
entries; a crashed prover delivered no verdict about anything, including the retained net, so
serial-after-one-re-probe is the correct-first floor. The repair obligation here is the re-probe and
the honest label, not a forced co-open.

### The normal site's degrade carries its classified cause

`serialDegradeReason` is now populated on the group-attempt-failed branch with
`overlapping_write_sets` or `parallel_safe_indeterminate`. Previously "label absent" was shared by
four causes (legs off, fewer than two writers, a proven overlap, a crashed prover) and therefore
distinguished nothing.

The legs-off and `<2`-writer branches, and the operator's `<2` write-cap ceiling, stay **unlabeled** —
those are ordinary serial, not degrades. The field is additive; an absent field keeps its existing
meaning.

## Consequences

- A persistently failing prover is now **visible** instead of masquerading as a task property.
- A transient fault is **repaired** instead of costing the frontier its parallelism.
- `overlapping_write_sets` now means what it says: somebody proved an overlap.
