# D-1050-01. Pass-rate posterior accept/reject for `metric-optimizer`

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: Accepted
Issue: #1050
Related: #634/D-634-01 (the original `metric-optimizer` role and median-of-K ratchet);
         #639/D-639-01 (OPT freeze-rule hardening; unchanged here)

## Context

The `metric-optimizer` contract accepted or rejected every iteration by comparing a median-of-K
point estimate to the running baseline with `direction` and `min_delta`. That rule remains sound
for continuous, low-noise metrics. It is a poor decision rule when the metric is a pass rate over
noisy trials: a point comparison on a handful of samples misjudges both directions, and the loop
always spends the full `metric_repeats` budget even on candidates that are already hopeless after
two or three trials.

Two empirical findings from Huxley-Gödel Machine (Wang, Piękos et al., arXiv:2510.21614v3) transfer
to this bounded ratchet: comparing Beta posteriors on noisy binary outcomes is more robust than
comparing empirical means with unequal sample counts, and deciding whether to evaluate a candidate
once more or move on lets the loop stop early on unpromising candidates. Everything else in that
paper (clade metaproductivity, Thompson sampling over an archive, UCB-Air expansion) is out of
scope: it would require a run-level ledger, which `_rules.md` and ADR 0017 refuse.

This is a wording-level change to one uncommon role, not a script, gate, or runtime mechanism.
It is not backed by an observed misjudgment in a Kaola run; it is built because the owner filed
#1050 as the work to do.

## Decision

Keep steps 1–3 and 6 of the ratchet protocol unchanged. Split steps 4–5 by metric kind, chosen by
the orchestrator in the mission:

- **Continuous metric (default, unchanged).** Median-of-K, compare to baseline by `direction` and
  `min_delta`. Accept with `kw-opt iter <k>: <old> -> <new>`; reject with scoped
  `git restore --source=HEAD`; `git reset --hard` remains forbidden.
- **Pass-rate metric.** Track `(n_success, n_failure)` for candidate and baseline as
  `Beta(1 + n_success, 1 + n_failure)`. Accept only when the posterior probability that the
  candidate beats the baseline in `direction` meets a mission-supplied confidence (default 0.9
  when supplied) **and** the posterior median clears `min_delta`. Ties and insufficient evidence
  are rejects. After a mission-supplied minimum number of trials (default 3 when supplied),
  abandon early when the candidate's upper ε-quantile is already below the baseline's posterior
  median (mirror for `min`), logging `rejected (abandoned after <n> trials)`. `metric_repeats` is
  a ceiling, not a target. Re-measure the baseline at loop start and carry it forward on accept
  (posterior-vs-posterior). The Output Contract includes per-iteration trial counts on pass-rate
  runs.

The confidence threshold and early-abandonment minimum are mission inputs the orchestrator
supplies; the role never invents them. An ambiguous metric kind is a STOP.

No new script, CLI flag, ledger, or freeze rule. D-634-01 and D-639-01 are not rewritten.
`behavior_contract_version` for `metric-optimizer` becomes 2.

## Consequences

- A mission that does not declare a pass-rate metric gets today's continuous behavior.
- The loop can only get shorter on pass-rate work (early abandonment); it cannot exceed
  `metric_repeats`.
- Generated native profiles follow from `templates/agents/behavior-contracts.json` via
  `scripts/generate-agent-profiles.js`. Runtime installers, adapters, and edition architecture
  are untouched.
